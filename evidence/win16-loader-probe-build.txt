The standalone Win16 probe was cross-built with the locally provisioned Open Watcom toolchain. This records compilation only; the executable has not been run in the Windows guest.

```sh
WATCOM=/tmp/ow-linux-x64 OWBIN=binl64 ./tests/win16/build-loader-probe.sh
```
